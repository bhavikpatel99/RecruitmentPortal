Running hire-to-close on the Recruitment Portal

One system covers the whole recruit-to-hire lifecycle — from a hiring manager's manpower request through screening, interviews, offers, and the handoff to onboarding. This guide walks through it by role: what you'll see when you log in, and what each screen is for.

Getting started

Open the portal's URL in a browser — you'll land on Sign In. From there:

  • Candidates create their own account. Click Register on the sign-in page and fill in your full name, email, and a password.
  • Staff and agency accounts are issued by an Admin — there's no self-service sign-up for Recruiter, Hiring Manager, or Agency accounts. If you need one, ask your Admin.
  • After signing in, you land on the page for your role automatically — see Roles at a glance below.
  • There's no self-service "forgot password" flow yet — if you're locked out, an Admin needs to help.
Password requirements At least 8 characters, including an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, and a digit. This applies whether you're registering yourself or an Admin is creating your account.
If you get signed out unexpectedly Sessions last a couple of hours. If yours expires while you're working, you'll see a clear "Your session has expired — please log in again" notice and be returned to Sign In — nothing is lost except needing to log back in.

Roles at a glance

What you can do — and where you land after logging in — depends on your role. An administrator sets this when your account is created.

RoleLands onCan do
Admin/workspace/dashboardEverything a Recruiter can, plus Org Setup, SMTP configuration, agency accounts, and every approval.
Recruiter/workspace/dashboardRuns day-to-day hiring: requisitions, postings, pipeline, screening, interviews, offers, communication, referrals.
Hiring Manager/workspace/dashboardRaises requisitions, joins interview panels, weighs in on offers and referrals.
Candidate/dashboardBrowses open roles, applies, tracks their own applications.
Agency/agency-portalA separate, restricted portal: submit candidates against open jobs, see submission status.
One rule worth knowing Every screen in the recruiting workspace checks your role on the server, not just in the menu — so if a tab is missing, it's not for your role, not a bug.

Finding & applying to jobs

Candidate
/jobs · /candidate/new · /dashboard

The public career site lists every open role. Opening a job shows the full description; Apply carries you into the application form with the role pre-filled.

  • The application form captures your profile once — contact details, experience, current/expected CTC, notice period, skills, and links to your LinkedIn, portfolio, or GitHub.
  • Resume: either upload the file directly (PDF, DOC, or DOCX — 5 MB max) or paste a link if it's hosted elsewhere. Uploading a file replaces any link you'd pasted.
  • Your Dashboard lists everything you've applied to with a live status badge, and lets you edit an application or withdraw it while it's still open.
  • If someone referred you, they'll send a link containing ?ref= and a code — apply through it and you're automatically credited to them, no extra step on your end.

Dashboard

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/dashboard

The landing page for the recruiting workspace — built to surface what needs attention rather than just totals.

  • Stat tiles: open jobs, requisitions pending your approval, active candidates, interviews scheduled today.
  • A hiring funnel showing how many candidates sit at each stage right now.
  • Recently applied candidates and your next few upcoming interviews, both one click from full detail.

Requisitions

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/requisitions

Every open role starts as a manpower request. + New Requisition asks for the title, department, location, vacancies, priority, whether it's a new seat or a replacement, a budget reference, and required skills — it saves as Draft.

  • From Draft, Submit moves it to Pending Approval; an approver clicks Approve or Reject (with a reason).
  • An approved requisition can be put On Hold and later Reopened, or Closed once it's filled.
  • Every transition is timestamped — click Audit on any requisition to see exactly who did what, and when.

Job Postings

RecruiterAdmin
/workspace/jobs

Once a requisition is approved, publish the actual job. Beyond the basics, the Job Description Builder section separates must-have skills from nice-to-have, and adds work mode, education requirement, benefits, and equal-opportunity/legal text.

  • Publish records which channels a role has gone out on — career site, LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri, referral, or other — each with its own status and optional expiry date.
  • Pipeline jumps straight to that job's kanban board; Close retires the posting once it's filled.

Pipeline

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/pipeline

Pick a job and drag candidate cards between stage columns. Every move is written to that candidate's history with who made it and, optionally, why.

Applied→ Screening→ Shortlisted→ Interview→ Assessment→ Final Evaluation→ Offer→ Hired

Clicking a card opens the full Candidate Detail page rather than a quick preview — the pipeline is for moving people, the detail page is for everything else.

Candidates & Talent Search

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/candidates · /workspace/talent-search · /workspace/talent-pools

The candidate detail page is the single record for one person, organized as tabs: Overview (profile, tags, recruiter notes), Pipeline, Screening, Assessments, Interviews, Offer, Onboarding, and Communication.

  • On Overview, you can upload or replace a candidate's resume file yourself (PDF, DOC, or DOCX — 5 MB max) — useful when one arrives by email rather than through the portal.
  • Talent Search filters everyone who's ever applied by keyword, skill, experience range, location, status, or tag — so a strong candidate from a closed role isn't lost.
  • Save a search you'll reuse, or add a candidate straight into a Talent Pool — a named shortlist you can return to for the next opening.

Screening & Shortlisting

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/screening-forms · Candidate Detail → Screening

Build a reusable screening form from Yes/No, text, numeric, or multiple-choice questions. Mark any question Knockout and set the expected answer.

What knockout does Run a screening on a candidate and answer a knockout question incorrectly — they're rejected automatically, with the reason logged to their history. No one has to remember to reject them manually.

For everyone who passes, review the answers and click Shortlist, Reject, or Hold — the pipeline stage updates to match.

Interviews

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/interviews · Candidate Detail → Interviews

Schedule Interview asks for the round, type (phone, technical, HR, panel, final), date and time, mode, a meeting link or location, and the panel — pick one interviewer as Lead.

  • Plans change: Reschedule or Cancel stay on the same interview record rather than starting over.
  • Each panelist submits their own scorecard: a 1–5 rating, a hire/no-hire recommendation, and free-text strengths, concerns, and comments.
  • Mark the round Completed or No Show once it's done.

Assessments

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/assessment-templates · Candidate Detail → Assessments

Define assessment templates once — type, vendor name, an external test link, a passing score, and expected duration — then reuse them across candidates.

  • Invite a candidate to an assessment and their stage moves to Assessment automatically.
  • Record Result compares the score to the template's passing mark: at or above it, the candidate advances to Final Evaluation; below it, they're rejected — both without a separate manual step.

Offers

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/offers · Candidate Detail → Offer

Create an offer with base salary, bonus, equity notes, benefits, and a validity date. It starts as Draft, then moves through Submit for Approval → Approve → Mark Sent — which emails the candidate.

  • If terms change, Log Negotiation updates the figures while keeping the full history of what was offered when.
  • Mark Accepted does three things at once: the candidate's stage becomes Offer Accepted, a pre-joining checklist is created for them automatically, and a hire record is queued for HR/ERP handoff.
  • Reject or Withdraw close it out the other way, each with its own audit trail.

Onboarding

RecruiterAdmin
Candidate Detail → Onboarding

Appears once an offer is accepted. Set the Joining Date, track Background Verification through Not Started → In Progress → Cleared (or Flagged), and check off the pre-joining checklist — ID proof, signed offer, certificates, relieving letter, BGV consent, bank details, IT access.

Handing off to HR Every acceptance queues a structured hire record for your HR/ERP system. Once that system has actually received it, mark it Sent to HR/ERP here so the handoff isn't left looking unfinished.

Communication Center

RecruiterAdmin
/workspace/communication

Three tabs. Templates holds the wording sent at each stage — application received, interview scheduled, assessment invitation, offer sent, and rejection — each written with placeholders like {{FirstName}}, {{JobTitle}}, and {{CompanyName}} that fill in automatically. Anyone can add a new template for a purpose the defaults don't cover.

  • SMTP Settings (Admin only) is where outgoing mail is configured — see Email & SMTP.
  • Send Log lists every email the system has attempted, with a Sent or Failed status and, on failure, the exact error — so a bounced message is never a mystery.

Agencies

RecruiterAdmin
/workspace/agencies

An Admin registers each recruiting agency and issues its portal login. From here, Recruiters and Admins see every candidate an agency has submitted, alongside per-agency performance — total submissions, duplicates caught, and hires.

See Agency Portal for what the agency itself sees.

Referrals

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/referrals

Choose an open job and click Generate Link — copy the resulting URL and send it to whoever you're referring. My Referrals shows every link you've shared and whether it's led to an application yet.

Bonus lifecycle The moment a referred candidate reaches Hired, their bonus status flips to Pending on its own. An Admin sets the amount and moves it to Approved, then Paid.

Analytics

RecruiterHiring ManagerAdmin
/workspace/analytics

Average time-to-fill and time-to-hire, offer acceptance rate, a funnel of how many candidates ever reached each stage, source effectiveness by channel, per-recruiter productivity, and how long interview panels take to return feedback.

Cost-per-hire and diversity metrics aren't shown — the system doesn't collect real budget actuals or protected-characteristic data, and a fabricated number is worse than none.

Org & Hiring Setup

Admin
/workspace/org-setup

The reference data every other screen's dropdowns pull from, across eight tabs:

  • Departments, Locations, Job Families, Skills, and Competencies — simple named lists.
  • Salary Bands — a min/max range per job family and level.
  • Approval Matrices — which role must sign off within a given amount range.
  • Interview Panels — a named, reusable group of interviewers, so you're not rebuilding the same panel for every requisition.

Email & SMTP

Admin
/workspace/communication → SMTP Settings

Outgoing mail runs through whatever SMTP account you configure here — Gmail, Zoho, Zimbra, Office 365, or any other server. Choosing a Provider preset fills in the host, port, and security mode for you; pick Custom to enter your own.

  • Enter the account's username, password, and the from-name/address you want candidates to see, then Save Settings.
  • Use Send Test to confirm it actually works before it's carrying real candidate mail.
Nothing sends until this is set Every automatic email — application received, interview scheduled, offer sent — depends on this being configured. Until it is, those attempts land in the Send Log marked Failed with the reason, rather than silently vanishing.

Local & demo setup

Admin

For showing the portal to a client without touching real data, two scripts under backend/Database/ spin up a completely separate, disposable database:

  • CreateFreshDemoDatabase.sql — creates a brand-new RecruitmentPortal_Demo database with the full schema. Your real database is never touched. Safe to re-run any time for a clean slate.
  • SeedDemoData.sql — fills it with realistic sample data: 3 job postings, 5 candidates at different pipeline stages, requisitions, and interviews with scorecards. Safe to re-run — it skips seeding if already done.

Seeded demo logins (all passwords work everywhere the portal checks password strength):

EmailPasswordRole
admin@recruitmentportal.comAdmin@123Admin
recruiter@recruitmentportal.comRecruiter@123Recruiter
hiringmanager@recruitmentportal.comManager@123Hiring Manager
candidate1/2/3@example.comCandidate@123Candidate

To point the app at the demo database instead of your real one, update the API's connection string to use RecruitmentPortal_Demo instead of RecruitmentPortal — either directly in appsettings.json, or (cleaner, since it's a one-line change to switch back) by setting the ConnectionStrings__DefaultConnection environment variable before starting the API.

Never run the main deploy script against real data Deploy.sql unconditionally drops and recreates whatever database it targets. It's meant for the very first setup, not for refreshing an environment with real hiring data in it — use the two demo scripts above for that instead.

Agency Portal

Agency
/agency-portal

A separate, deliberately smaller portal — agencies see open jobs and their own submissions, nothing else in the system.

  • Submit Candidate on any open job takes name, contact details, experience, current company, expected CTC, skills, and a resume link.
  • If that email already exists in the system — from a direct application, another agency, or a referral — the submission is flagged as a duplicate and no new record is created. Ownership stays with whoever got there first.
  • Your Submissions tracks every candidate you've put forward and their current status.

Future enhancements

Two pages in the product today look AI-powered but are illustrative previews, not live features — worth knowing so no one mistakes sample output for a real analysis.

Currently mock data Resume Analyzer (/resume-analyzer) pre-fills a sample resume and shows canned analysis — it does not read a candidate's actual uploaded resume. Interview Guidance (/interview-guidance) shows fixed tips, sample questions, and a checklist for a job title/department — it is not generated per candidate or per posting. Both are kept in the product as a preview of where AI could go, not as working features yet.

Planned AI enhancements (not yet built):

  • Real resume parsing — read an uploaded resume file (now that upload is supported) to auto-fill the application form or extract skills automatically.
  • AI-assisted resume-to-job matching score, so a recruiter can rank candidates against a specific posting instead of reading every profile in full.
  • AI-generated interview questions and guidance tailored to the actual job description and candidate profile, replacing today's fixed sample content.
  • AI-assisted screening summaries — a short written summary of a candidate's fit, useful when working through a large pipeline.

Other planned enhancements (not AI-related):

  • A self-service "forgot password" flow.
  • Cloud-based resume storage with malware scanning — resumes currently live on the API server's local disk.
  • SMS/WhatsApp candidate notifications alongside email.
  • Live job-board posting integrations and BGV vendor webhooks — currently tracked and logged in the system, but not wired to a real third-party service.
  • Cost-per-hire and diversity analytics, once real budget figures and (optionally, compliantly collected) demographic data exist to report on.

Pipeline stages, in order

The default flow a candidate moves through — organizations can add stages of their own, so treat this as the common path rather than a fixed list.

Applied→ Screening→ Shortlisted→ Interview→ Assessment→ Final Evaluation→ Offer→ Offer Accepted→ Pre-Joining→ Hired

A candidate can also land in one of four terminal states, at any point:

Rejected Withdrawn Offer Declined Position Closed

Tips & troubleshooting

A candidate was rejected and I didn't do itCheck their Pipeline tab's history. It's usually a failed knockout question in Screening, or an Assessment score below the template's passing mark — both reject automatically and log the reason.
Send Test in Communication Center does nothingSMTP Settings needs to be saved first, by an Admin — see Email & SMTP.
A menu item I expect isn't thereOrg Setup and SMTP Settings are Admin-only; Agencies is limited to Recruiters and Admins. It's a permissions boundary, not a missing feature.
Where do approval thresholds live?Org Setup → Approval Matrices records which role should sign off above a given amount, as a reference for whoever approves requisitions and offers.
I want to reuse a strong candidate for a different roleAdd them to a Talent Pool from Talent Search, or search across every past applicant directly — nothing is limited to the job they first applied for.
My resume upload was rejectedOnly PDF, DOC, and DOCX files up to 5 MB are accepted. Convert the file or paste a link instead if it's hosted elsewhere.
I was suddenly signed out mid-taskYour session expired (sessions last a couple of hours) — sign in again and pick up where you left off. This isn't an error; work you'd already saved isn't affected.
